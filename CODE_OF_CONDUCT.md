# Code of Conduct

We expect respectful, practical, and security-minded collaboration.

## Expected Behavior

- Respect different skill levels
- Share evidence with claims
- Keep discussions constructive

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or discrimination
- Misleading claims about wallet safety or returns
- Spam or abusive communication

## Enforcement

Maintainers may moderate, remove, or restrict participation when needed to protect the project and community.
